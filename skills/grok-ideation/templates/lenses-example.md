# Example lenses (from a 36-hour ETHGlobal run; write fresh ones for each event)

Each lens is one line, appended to the shared brief and sent as its own Grok chat. Good lenses force different
ideas. Prize stacks, a "hard thing", a place, a primitive and a market type pull in five different directions.

L1 | LENS: Max money on <chain>. Stack <prize A> + <prize B> + <prize C> in ONE product where all three are load-bearing, not bolted on. Go crazy.
L2 | LENS: What <sponsor>'s newest feature (<feature>) makes possible that was IMPOSSIBLE before. Things that could not be built last month.
L3 | LENS: One hard, verifiable thing that makes judges say WOW: a ZK, cryptography, measurement or systems feat nobody has done, demoable live.
L4 | LENS: A Gen-Z consumer app for <city/country> that goes viral there (<local culture: sport, films, transit, payments habits>). Crypto is invisible.
L5 | LENS: A NEW agent primitive. Not budgets or spend caps, not human-approval routing, not marketplaces, not identity registries. Something agents can't do today.
L6 | LENS: New market types: markets for things that aren't tokens (attention, time, compute, weather, reputation, blockspace).
L7 | LENS: The under-subscribed track. <track> is likely to get few entries; win it outright with a real business use.
L8 | LENS: Emotional, real-life stakes: death, inheritance, family money, health, safety. A demo moment a non-technical judge FEELS.
L9 | LENS: The sponsor's own pain. What do <sponsor>'s users complain about on X and in Discord this month? Fix that.
L10 | LENS: Anti-idea. List the 5 ideas every other team will build for this event, then invert each one into something nobody will.
L11 | LENS: Crazy but needed. What would make a judge laugh, then realise it solves a real pain? Rule out "existing product + a new gate" (in one round, 9 of Grok's 10 ideas were "X + World ID").
